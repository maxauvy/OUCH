OUCH
Free · open source · no account

Your pain:
what makes it worse,
what eases it.
Then you act.

OUCH is a diary for fibromyalgia and other chronic pain. You log your day in under a minute. Over the weeks, your notes show what seems to make your pain worse and what helps, so you can act on it.

What it’s for
01

See what makes it worse

Sleep, stress, yesterday’s activity: OUCH compares your days and shows what comes back when things get harder. One lead at a time, with the days behind it.

02

See what helps

A short walk, a hot bath, stretching… whatever you noted as helping, with your days with and without it side by side.

03

Try it for a week

A lead speaks to you? Start a trial: you log as usual and OUCH compares your days with and without. It proves nothing, but it helps you see more clearly.

04

Talk it over with your doctor

The leads you keep go into the report (2 pages, or 5 for a pain clinic), with your curve and your treatments. It describes your data without drawing conclusions for you.

05

Even on days with no energy

When things are hard, the screen shrinks to your pain. One number is enough: the diary keeps going.

The questions
we get asked.

What is it for, exactly?

To spot what makes your pain worse and what helps, so you can act on it and talk it over with your doctor. OUCH shows you leads drawn from your notes, and you can try them for a week to see.

Will it take much time?

Under a minute: one number from 0 to 10 is enough. The rest is optional, and the screen shrinks on harder days.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Free, no subscription, no ads, and no account. The code is open: anyone can read it.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your phone, unless you turn on automatic weather or Dropbox backup (trial). A code lock is available.

What if I change phones?

You export an encrypted backup with a password of your choice, then import it on the new one. The app regularly reminds you to make a backup.

Do I need an app store?

No. You open it in your browser, then “Add to Home Screen”. After that it works offline too.

Is it medical advice?

No. OUCH describes your notes and diagnoses nothing. A lead can also run the other way round: people do more on the days they feel better.

What about children?

A tab explains your day to them in gentle, simple words, for three ages: 4–7, 8–12 and 13–17.

Not sure where to start?

Launch the demo: 90 made-up days and a guided tour. You see everything without entering a thing.

Is it available in French?

Yes, and so is the report. You can switch language any time.

Want a look?

Open the link, then choose “Discover with an example”. Nothing to install, nothing to create.
