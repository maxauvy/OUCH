Your pain diary,one minute a day.

OUCH is made for fibromyalgia and other chronic pain. You log your day in under a minute. Over the weeks, your notes show what seems to make your pain worse and what helps, so you can act on it and talk it over with your doctor. No account: your data stays on your phone.

  2. 2The harder daysWhen the last few days have been hard, the screen gets lighter and reminds you of what has helped before. Logging your pain is enough.
  3. 3One number from 0 to 10Your pain over the whole day, not just right now. Sleep, fatigue, mood, body areas, treatments: everything else is optional.
  4. 4Tabs to go furtherYour journal as a calendar, your trends, Relief to breathe slowly or let go, and Consultation to get ready for your appointment.

What your notes say

OUCH compares your days and shows what comes back when things get harder, or easier: sleep, stress, a short walk… One lead at a time, with the days behind it. A lead puts your days side by side: on its own, it proves nothing.

Try it for a week

A lead speaks to you? Start a trial: you log as usual, and OUCH compares your days with and without. It helps you see more clearly, without promising anything.

For your appointment, and for your loved ones

Your doctor

A report to show

Your curve, key figures, treatments and questions, in a family doctor or pain clinic version. It describes your data without drawing conclusions for you.

Your loved ones

Simple words

Your weather of the day to share, or for them to follow daily in OUCH Duo, and a tab that explains your day to children aged 4 to 17 (“today, Mom’s sky is a bit cloudy”).

Keep in mind

  • Your data stays on your phone.
  • An encrypted backup, with your own password.
  • OUCH describes your notes and diagnoses nothing.
  • It does not replace your doctor.
